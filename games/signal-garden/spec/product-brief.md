# Signal Garden Product Brief

`Signal Garden` is a tiny original tower-defense/factory hybrid. The player grows signal plants
that produce charge, routes charge into simple towers, and survives short static storms.

Target first playable: 1-2 weeks after `MyEngine` v0.1 hardening.

Out of scope: multiplayer, campaign, monetization, automation graphs larger than one resource
chain, and reference-game cloning.
